How to Submit Your Music to Blogs and Magazines for Free

Music PR basics · 5 min read

Many artists search for "how to send music to blogs" and end up paying for services before trying the free route. You can reach real editors without spending anything, provided you send the right material to the right people.

1. Pick outlets that match your genre

Read a few recent posts before you pitch. If a blog covers indie folk and you make drum and bass, your email will go nowhere no matter how good the track is.

2. Send a complete, easy package

  • A streaming link (Spotify, Bandcamp or SoundCloud) that works without a login
  • A short bio and a two-sentence release description
  • High-quality press photos with a photographer credit
  • Your social links so outlets can tag you

3. Keep the pitch short

Editors receive a lot of email. Lead with who you are, what the song is and why it fits their site, in under 150 words. Attachments are risky, so use links.

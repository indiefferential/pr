Music Blog Submission Etiquette: Dos and Don'ts

Music PR basics · 4 min read

Small mistakes get pitches ignored. These habits separate artists who get replies from those who don't.

Do

  • Address the editor by name when you can find it
  • Mention a recent article of theirs that relates to your sound
  • Send one clear streaming link and one photo link
  • Say thank you, whether or not they cover you

Don't

  • Send mass emails with everyone's address visible
  • Attach large WAV files or zip folders
  • Demand coverage or a reply date
  • Pitch the same track repeatedly

Treat editors as people who choose what to cover, not as a service you are owed. A polite, well-prepared pitch is remembered.

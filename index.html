How It Works

1. Submit for Free

Fill out one simple form with your artist information, bio, release details, social links and photos. No signup or fees required.

2. We Pitch to Media

Our PR editors review submissions and send selected stories to journalists, blogs, magazines, playlists and digital media outlets.

3. Approval & Email Notification

Once approved, you'll receive an email notification before your press materials are shared by one or more outlets in our network.

Why BRiNSPOT Is Free

Every artist deserves access to real PR opportunities without paying thousands to agencies. BRiNSPOT is supported by donations and sponsors, allowing free submissions for all artists. We only grow when your story gets seen.
